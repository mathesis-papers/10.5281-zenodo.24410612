Oudra's cubic and quadratic floor conjectures and an alternative proof of Wu's tetrahedral formula

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordDOILean source

Abstract

We prove Ridouane Oudra's cubic floor conjecture, posed on October 6, 2023 in the FORMULA field of OEIS A248186, revision 18, and give an alternative proof of the tetrahedral counting formula contributed by Chai Wah Wu to A056556 on November 4, 2024 and treated in his 2025 article, Section 4, p.22. The least positive index k for which 1/18-sum_{h=1}^k 1/[h(h+1)(h+2)(h+3)]<n^(-3) is floor(r*n+r^2/n-1) for n>2, where r=3^(-1/3). An integer-target cubic inversion also recovers Oudra's earlier tetrahedral formula. A congruence argument proves his quadratic conjecture in A248183. For both threshold sequences we obtain exact jump positions and complementary densities, and for the cubic jumps we characterize their deviations from the associated Beatty sequence.
